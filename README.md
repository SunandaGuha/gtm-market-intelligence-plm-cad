**Author:** Sunanda Guha

---

## 📌 Project Overview & Business Context

In the highly competitive Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) and Computer-Aided Design (CAD) industry, organizations must continuously identify growth opportunities, understand competitive dynamics, and prioritize market segments for investment.

This project delivers an **AI-Powered Go-To-Market (GTM) Market Intelligence Framework** that combines market analysis, customer sentiment analysis, competitor intelligence, and revenue forecasting to support strategic decision-making.

Using **Google Gemini**, **DistilBERT NLP**, **Python analytics**, and **Tableau dashboards**, the project identifies underserved customer segments, evaluates competitive positioning, and quantifies revenue opportunities across the PLM/CAD market.

The final deliverable is an executive-ready dashboard designed to help business leaders prioritize GTM investments and maximize revenue potential.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

### Phase 1: Market Intelligence Analysis
- Market segmentation
- Competitor landscape assessment
- AI-assisted competitor research using Gemini
- Opportunity identification

### Phase 2: Customer Sentiment Analysis

Customer reviews are analyzed using DistilBERT NLP models to:
- Classify sentiment
- Identify recurring customer pain points
- Detect dissatisfaction trends
- Discover market opportunities

### Phase 3: GTM Opportunity Scoring

A custom weighted scoring model prioritizes customer segments using:

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---|
| Market Size | 25% |
| Growth Rate | 25% |
| Competitive Gap | 20% |
| Customer Pain Points | 15% |
| Strategic Fit | 15% |

This framework enables objective prioritization of GTM investments.

### Phase 4: Revenue Forecasting

Revenue opportunities are projected across a three-year horizon by combining:
- Market growth assumptions
- Segment attractiveness
- Estimated market capture rates

### Phase 5: Executive Dashboard Development

Insights are visualized in Tableau to support executive decision-making and strategic planning.
